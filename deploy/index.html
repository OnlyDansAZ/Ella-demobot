What Ella Can Do For You

Ella isn't just another AI bot. She's a comprehensive sales assistant capable of handling your entire outbound sales process with human-like conversations.

Outbound Sales Calls

Ella makes natural phone calls on your behalf, navigating complex conversations, managing objections, qualifying leads, and booking appointments.

Text Conversations

Engage prospects through personalized SMS conversations that feel authentic and responsive, with no AI-sounding templates.

Appointment Setting

Ella schedules meetings directly on your calendar, handles rescheduling, and sends reminder notifications to maximize show rates.

Smart Follow-ups

Never lose a lead with perfectly timed follow-up sequences that adapt to prospect engagement patterns.

Sales Intelligence

Gain actionable insights with comprehensive analytics on conversation performance, objection patterns, and conversion rates.

Advanced Memory

Ella remembers every interaction with prospects and uses that context to have more meaningful conversations over time.

How Ella Works

Getting started with Ella is simple - she integrates seamlessly with your existing workflow in just a few steps.

Upload Your Contacts

Import your leads from CSV, your CRM, or manually enter prospect information into the dashboard.

Customize Your Campaign

Set up your campaign goals, conversation style, and specific talking points for Ella to cover.

Launch Your Outreach

Activate your campaign and let Ella begin reaching out to prospects via calls or texts.

Monitor & Optimize

Track results in real-time and refine your approach based on performance analytics.
