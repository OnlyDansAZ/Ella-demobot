## Customizing Ella's Appearance

### Changing Themes
Ella offers several built-in themes to personalize your experience:

1. **Default** - The standard YoBot theme with a clean, professional blue look
2. **Modern Blue** - A sleek and modern blue theme with light accents
3. **Night Mode** - Dark theme with vibrant purple accents for low-light environments
4. **Natural Green** - Calm and natural green theme inspired by nature
5. **Sunset Orange** - Warm and energetic theme with sunset-inspired colors

To change themes:
1. Click the "Customize Theme" button in the settings panel
2. Select the "Color Themes" tab
3. Choose your preferred theme by clicking on it
4. Your selection is automatically saved and will persist between sessions

### Adjusting Message Appearance
You can customize how messages appear in your conversations with Ella:

1. **Message Bubble Opacity** - Adjust the transparency of message bubbles (30-100%)
2. **User Message Color** - Toggle between using the theme's primary color for your messages or a standard gray

To adjust message appearance:
1. Open the "Customize Theme" panel
2. Select the "Appearance" tab
3. Use the opacity slider to adjust message transparency
4. Toggle "Use Primary Color for My Messages" on or off

### Font Size and Animations
You can adjust text size and animation settings:

1. **Font Size** - Scale the text size up or down throughout the interface
2. **Animations** - Enable or disable micro-animations for a more dynamic or static experience

To modify these settings:
1. Open the "Customize Theme" panel
2. Select the "Animations" tab for animation toggles
3. Use the "Font Size" slider in the Appearance tab to adjust text size

## Using Different Personas

### Available Personas
Ella comes with several predefined personas, each with its own communication style and focus:

1. **Default Ella** - Friendly, helpful AI assistant for general use
2. **Sales Assistant** - Focused on explaining YoBot's offerings and guiding users to the right tier
3. **Technical Support** - More detailed and technical in explanations
4. **Executive Assistant** - More formal and business-oriented
5. **Casual Helper** - More conversational and relaxed

To switch personas:
1. Click the "Persona" dropdown in the settings panel
2. Select your desired persona from the list
3. Your conversation will continue with the selected persona

### Creating Custom Personas
You can create your own custom personas to tailor Ella's behavior precisely to your needs:

1. Click "Create Custom Persona" in the persona dropdown
2. Enter a name for your persona
3. Write instructions that define how Ella should behave
4. Consider specifying:
   - Communication style and tone
   - Areas of focus or expertise
   - How to handle certain types of questions
   - Any special knowledge or emphasis

Your custom personas will be saved for future use.

## Voice Interaction

### Speaking to Ella
Ella can understand your voice input:

1. Click the microphone icon to begin voice input
2. Allow microphone permissions if prompted
3. Speak clearly into your microphone
4. Your speech will be automatically transcribed and sent to Ella
5. Click the microphone icon again to stop listening

For best results:
- Speak in a quiet environment
- Use clear, complete sentences
- Wait for the microphone indication before speaking

### Listening to Ella
Ella can respond to you with natural-sounding speech:

1. Adjust the volume using the volume slider in the settings panel
2. Click the mute/unmute button to toggle audio responses
3. Audio will play automatically when Ella responds

## Scheduling and Appointments
Ella can help you manage your calendar and appointments:

1. Ask Ella to schedule a meeting or appointment
2. Ella will guide you through selecting a date and time
3. Specify details such as meeting purpose, duration, and items to bring
4. View upcoming appointments by clicking the "Appointments" button
5. Ella will remind you about scheduled appointments at appropriate times

When scheduling:
- Be specific about date and time preferences
- Mention any specific preparation or items needed
- Ella will check for conflicts with existing appointments
