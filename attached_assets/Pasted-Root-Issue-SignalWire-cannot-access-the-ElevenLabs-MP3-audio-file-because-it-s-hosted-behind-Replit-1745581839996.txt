Root Issue:
SignalWire cannot access the ElevenLabs MP3 audio file because it’s hosted behind Replit’s environment, which does not serve files in a production-grade, globally accessible way.

Even though you can hit the MP3 URL from your browser, SignalWire’s servers may be geofenced, blocked by Replit’s CDN, or hitting CORS/security restrictions. This is 100% the most common reason why <Play> disconnects immediately.

✅ Your Cleanest Fix Path (No More Call Drops):
💾 Step 1: Move audio hosting to a reliable, public-facing CDN or bucket
Recommended Options:

Amazon S3 (free-tier works)

Cloudflare R2

DigitalOcean Spaces

Even GitHub Pages (for static audio)
